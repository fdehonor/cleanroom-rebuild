# Public-use policy

The Cleanroom Rebuild installer and original plugin code are available at no charge under the MIT License. This does not override third-party licenses or grant rights in software submitted for analysis.

Use the workflow only when you own the target or have explicit authorization to inspect and reimplement it. Do not use it to bypass access controls, license checks, technical protection measures, account restrictions, or confidentiality obligations.

The release gate verifies engineering evidence. It does not determine legal compliance, ownership, non-infringement, patent clearance, export compliance, or freedom to operate. Organizations shipping a clone should obtain appropriate legal review.

The installer contacts GitHub, Apache, Adoptium, PyPI, npm, Microsoft/winget sources, and—when model weights are requested—Hugging Face. Review those services' terms and privacy practices before installation.

No warranty or support guarantee is provided. Do not analyze untrusted binaries on a machine containing sensitive data; use a disposable virtual machine or isolated workstation.
